About

About Built With Grok

Built with Grok is the UK’s first exclusively Grok AI consultancy that ships software and hardware experiences with Grok as the intelligence layer.

Mission: Help ambitious teams turn AI ambition into shipped products— digital and physical—using Grok as the intelligence layer.

Demand for AI services is growing faster than raw model revenue alone. Enterprises and startups still fail most GenAI pilots not for lack of models, but for lack of product strategy, integration, and delivery. We close that gap.

We are Grok-first and integration-ready: deep patterns for Grok APIs, agents, voice, and real-time context—without locking you into dogma when another model is required. We are not a foundation-model lab, a pure strategy-deck shop, or a body shop for undifferentiated glue code.

Vision: Become the default product studio for Grok-powered experiences— from agents and apps to smart connected products.

  • Primary ICP: founders, CTOs, Heads of Product, innovation leads
  • Seed–Series C and mid-market innovators with project budget
  • Remote-first delivery across English-speaking markets
  • Outcomes over hours; written ship definitions
  • Client owns core product IP by default
