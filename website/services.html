Services & products

Three flagship offers. Grok-first. Fixed scope where it counts. Client owns core product IP by default.

1. Grok Product Sprint

Turn a well-scoped idea into a working digital MVP—app, agent, workflow, or API-backed experience— using Grok as the intelligence layer, in a fixed window with clear acceptance criteria.

£25k–£80k 2–6 weeks Digital MVP

You get

  • Light product framing & core UX happy path
  • Grok-powered features (chat, agents, voice, or structured generation)
  • Frontend + backend for agreed MVP scope
  • Staging deploy, basic auth, handoff docs

Best for

  • Founders & CTOs who need something shippable
  • Internal copilots and customer-facing agents
  • Vertical workflows (support, research, content)
  • Companion software ahead of a hardware path

2. Production AI Systems

Take a working demo or Sprint MVP and make it production-grade: reliable agents and tools, observability, cost control, security baselines, and launch support.

£50k–£250k+ 4–16 weeks phased Hardening & launch

You get

  • Architecture review & hardening plan
  • Agent / tool / RAG quality improvements
  • Observability, usage budgets, rate limits
  • Eval harness, runbooks, cutover support

Best for

  • Teams past “it works in a demo”
  • Wider internal or customer rollout
  • Cost and reliability under real load
  • Post-Sprint launch path

3. Physical × AI Lab

Design and prototype physical products with Grok-backed experiences—connected devices, smart products, installations—without standing up a full hardware org. We own product sense and AI UX; we orchestrate design, electronics, and pilot manufacturing partners.

£40k–£200k+ BOM often pass-through Concept → prototype

You get

  • Concept & experience design (physical + digital)
  • Architecture: sensors, connectivity, cloud, Grok layer
  • Companion app / voice / agent MVP
  • Demo-ready prototype + pilot manufacturing brief

Best for

  • Brands adding AI + physical SKUs
  • Retail / interactive installations
  • Operator tools with edge + cloud intelligence
  • Teams that need a path, not a factory
