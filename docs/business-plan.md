## 1. Executive summary

**Built With Grok** is an AI product consultancy and studio that designs, builds, and ships **digital and physical products** with **Grok / xAI** as the primary intelligence layer. The market for AI *services and productization* is expanding as companies move past chat demos toward production systems—and fail when strategy, integration, and delivery lag the model.

BWG wins by:

1. **Grok-native depth** — agents, voice, real-time context, tooling, and cost/latency craft—not shallow multi-model wrappers.  
2. **Shipping bias** — fixed-scope sprints and production hardening; demos are milestones, customers are the goal.  
3. **Digital + physical** — software products *and* connected/smart product programs orchestrated with hardware partners.  
4. **Founder- and innovation-team friendly packaging** — clear offers in the mid five- to low six-figure range, optional retainers and co-build.

**Near-term model:** Services-led revenue (sprints, production builds, physical×AI programs, retainers) with path to productized IP and joint ventures.

**Year-1 ambition (directional):** Prove 2–4 lighthouse ships, establish brand and pipeline, reach sustainable founder-led revenue with a small core team + specialist bench.

## 3. Market opportunity

### 3.1 Opportunity thesis

- AI spend continues to grow; **services, integration, and productization** capture a large share as buyers need outcomes, not only API access.  
- Pilot-to-production gap remains wide → demand for teams that **ship**.  
- Frontier landscape includes OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, and **xAI/Grok**; specialization on Grok is a wedge while remaining integration-ready.  
- Few competitors credibly combine **Grok-deep software** with **physical product** orchestration.

### 3.2 Target market (ICP)

**Primary (near-term revenue)**

- Founders, CTOs, Heads of Product, Innovation Leads  
- Seed–Series C startups; SMB–mid-market innovators; corporate innovation pods  
- ~10–500 employees (or small pods in larger orgs)  
- Budget for **£25k–£150k** engagements or multi-month retainers  
- Want a **product**, not a 200-page roadmap  

**Secondary (later / larger)**

- Enterprise Grok-specific POCs with production path  
- Brands needing companion apps + physical experiences  
- Industrial/logistics edge AI (partner-heavy)

**Anti-ICP (early):** Strategy-only buyers; pure staff-aug with no product ownership; day-one multi-year regulated programs without partners; clients who reject any path to Grok/xAI when that is core to the offer.

### 3.3 Competitive position

| vs. | Our edge |
|-----|----------|
| Big consultancies | Faster, specialist, product-shipping bias, lower overhead |
| Generic AI agencies | Grok depth + physical lane + outcome packaging |
| Hardware houses | Stronger AI product sense and software loop |
| Freelancers | Accountable end-to-end delivery and brand |

**Positioning one-liner:**  
*Built With Grok helps ambitious teams ship real products—software and hardware—using Grok as the intelligence layer, from concept to customer.*

## 4. Offerings & business model

### 4.1 Revenue model

| Stream | Description | Cadence |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Fixed-scope projects** | Sprints, production phases, physical prototype programs | Project |
| **Retainers** | Continuous iteration, model upgrades, ops | Monthly |
| **Discovery / workshops** | Paid scoping that feeds projects | Project |
| **Co-build / rev-share** (selective) | Joint products where incentives align | Milestone + upside |
| **Productized IP** (future) | Templates, vertical SKUs, internal tools spun from delivery | License / SaaS |

**Primary early revenue:** Fixed-scope + retainers. Co-build only with clear IP and success criteria.

### 4.2 Flagship offer lines (to be formalized as three products/services next)

Research and this plan converge on three flagships for go-to-market packaging:

1. **Grok Product Sprint** — Digital MVP / agent / app in a fixed window (typically 2–6 weeks).  
2. **Production AI Systems** — Hardening, RAG/tools/agents, observability, cost control, launch support.  
3. **Physical × AI Lab** — Concept → prototype → pilot manufacturing path for smart/connected products with Grok-backed UX (apps, voice, agents).

Supporting: Discovery workshops and monthly retainers as attach/upsell motions.

### 4.3 Pricing framework (indicative USD; validate in market)

| Offer | Indicative range | Notes |
|-------|------------------|-------|
| Discovery workshop | £5,000–£15,000 | 1–2 weeks; requirements, architecture sketch, roadmap |
| Grok Product Sprint | £25,000–£80,000 | Scope-boxed MVP; clear acceptance criteria |
| Production build phase | £50,000–£250,000+ | Depends on integrations, SLAs, compliance |
| Physical × AI prototype program | £40,000–£200,000+ | BOM and tooling often client-borne or pass-through |
| Monthly product retainer | £8,000–£30,000 | Capacity + roadmap ownership |

**Margin levers:** Reusable Grok agent patterns, design systems, deployment templates, partner manufacturing relationships, productized accelerators.

### 4.4 Commercial principles

- **Outcomes over hours** — fixed scope where possible; time & materials only when discovery risk is high.  
- **Grok-first, integration-ready** — prefer Grok; multi-model when client constraints require it.  
- **IP clarity** — client owns core product IP by default; BWG retains internal tools/accelerators unless negotiated.  
- **No endless pilots** — every engagement has a ship definition.

## 5. Go-to-market (GTM)

### 5.1 Phase 0–1 (0–6 months): Founder-led

| Channel | Action |
|---------|--------|
| **Outbound** | Direct to founders & product leaders in AI-curious verticals (consumer, media, logistics, education, creative tools) |
| **Content / build-in-public** | Ship demos; “Built with Grok” case notes; X + LinkedIn narrative |
| **Inbound site** | Clear offers, proof, about, contact (marketing site in repo) |
| **Network** | Warm intros; design partners for lighthouse projects |
| **Partnerships** | Early conversations with xAI ecosystem, CM/electronics partners, cloud/infra where useful |

**Goal:** 2–4 lighthouse projects; public artifacts; repeatable sales narrative.

### 5.2 Phase 2 (6–18 months): Repeatable pipeline

- Case studies with metrics (time-to-launch, cost/interaction, conversion, uptime).  
- Partner channel (agencies referring hardware; hardware firms referring AI).  
- Workshops and templates as lead magnets.  
- Selective vertical packaging (e.g. media agents, ops copilots, companion devices).

### 5.3 Sales motion

1. **Qualify** against ICP and anti-ICP.  
2. **Paid discovery** or structured scoping call → written proposal.  
3. **Sprint or phase contract** with milestones and acceptance criteria.  
4. **Launch + retainer** or next phase (production / physical pilot).  

**Cycle time target:** First serious conversation → signed sprint in 2–4 weeks for primary ICP.

### 5.4 Messaging

- **Headline:** Products, not pilots. Built with Grok.  
- **Subhead:** We help teams design and launch AI-powered software and physical products—using Grok as the intelligence layer.  
- **Tone:** Direct, builder-culture, ambitious; aligned with Grok energy without brand confusion.

## 6. Operations & delivery

### 6.1 Delivery lifecycle

```
Inquiry → Qualify → Discovery/Scoping → Proposal → Build (sprint/phases)
  → Launch → Handoff / Retainer → Case study (with permission)
```

### 6.2 Core capabilities

| Capability | How we cover it |
|------------|-----------------|
| Product strategy & UX | Core team / design partners |
| Full-stack software | Core eng |
| Grok / agent systems | Core AI product engineers |
| Voice / real-time / multimodal | Patterns + platform APIs |
| DevOps / observability / cost | Templates and production playbooks |
| Hardware ID / electronics / firmware | Partner network + BWG product lead |
| Manufacturing pilot | Contract manufacturers; client or BWG-orchestrated |

### 6.3 Team model (lean)

| Role | Early stage |
|------|-------------|
| Founder / product lead | Sales, scoping, quality bar |
| AI / full-stack engineers | Delivery |
| Design (product/UX) | Staff or retain |
| Hardware partners | As-needed for Physical × AI |
| Ops / finance | Lightweight (founder + tools) |

Scale with contractors and specialists before heavy fixed overhead.

### 6.4 Tools & stack (illustrative)

- **Intelligence:** Grok / xAI APIs (primary); others when required  
- **Delivery:** Modern web/app stack, agent frameworks as chosen per project  
- **Ops:** Project tracker, shared docs, Git-based delivery, client Slack/email  
- **Hardware:** Partner CAD/ECAD, prototyping labs, CMs  

### 6.5 Quality bar

- Written scope and acceptance criteria  
- Staging environments and basic observability for production work  
- Security and data-handling notes appropriate to client  
- Documentation and handoff, not “knowledge hostage”

## 7. Financial outline

### 7.1 Assumptions (directional, not audited forecast)

- Services-led; low inventory (except optional prototype materials)  
- High gross margin on pure software work; hardware programs have lower margin on pass-through BOM  
- Founder-led sales; modest marketing spend early  

### 7.2 Revenue scenarios (Year 1 illustrative)

| Scenario | Projects / retainers (rough) | Approx. revenue |
|----------|------------------------------|-----------------|
| **Conservative** | 3 sprints + 1 small production + light retainer | ~£150k–£250k |
| **Base** | 5–7 projects mix + 1–2 retainers | ~£350k–£600k |
| **Upside** | Strong lighthouse + enterprise attach + retainer stack | ~£700k–£1.2M+ |

Actuals depend on close rates, average deal size, and capacity.

### 7.3 Cost structure (early)

| Category | Notes |
|----------|--------|
| Labor (core + contractors) | Primary cost |
| Tools / APIs / cloud | Project-variable + fixed SaaS |
| Marketing / site / content | Modest; time-heavy |
| Legal / insurance / admin | Entity, contracts, E&O as needed |
| Hardware prototypes | Client-funded or milestone-billed preferred |

### 7.4 Unit economics targets

- **Gross margin (software projects):** 50–70%+ after delivery labor  
- **Retainer utilization:** Sustainable capacity planning (avoid 100% billable burnout)  
- **CAC:** Prefer network + content over paid until messaging is proven  
- **Cash:** Prefer deposits (e.g. 30–50% up front) and milestone billing  

### 7.5 Funding stance

Default: **bootstrap / revenue-funded**. Raise only if a clear scale bet (productized SKU, geographic expansion, or heavy physical lab) requires capital beyond operating cash.

## 8. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| xAI API pricing, access, or ToS shifts | Integration-ready patterns; multi-model fallback; monitor official docs |
| Hardware supply chain complexity | Partner network; start low-BOM; pass-through costs |
| Crowded AI consulting noise | Sharp Grok + physical wedge; public ships; case metrics |
| Talent scarcity (AI + hardware) | Core team + vetted bench; clear delivery playbooks |
| Brand dependency on “Grok” | Trademark/relationship hygiene; “BWG” secondary brand |
| Scope creep | Fixed-scope contracts; change-order discipline |
| Client concentration | Pipeline diversity; retainers as base |
